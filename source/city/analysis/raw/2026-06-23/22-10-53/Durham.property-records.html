Real Estate

Duties & Responsibilities


Real Estate provides services through real estate sales, acquisition, and property management. For all City departments, the division also prepares feasibility studies, comparative market analyses, and manages the City Tract database.


Services


  • Affordable Housing Programs
  • Disposition of surplus City-owned real property
  • Economic Development Initiatives
  • Feasibility Studies
  • Fire Stations
  • General Acquisition of Properties
  • Geological Surveys
  • Lake Michie Property Transactions
  • Parks and Greenways
  • Police Substations
  • Property Management
  • Sidewalks
  • Street Rights of Way
  • Water and Sewer Easements

Links


  • Look up ownership details about any property located in Durham County at the Durham County Tax Administration search page: https://taxcama.dconc.gov/camapwa/
  • Look up specific Deed information at the Durham County Register of Deeds search page: https://rodweb.dconc.gov/web/user/disclaimer.
