# Association of East Asian Relations — national contacts: sources & references

This folder collects national-level contacts for **Association of East Asian Relations**. Data provenance
by role:

## Ambassador / diplomatic missions  (`ambassador.csv`)
- Source: **database-of-embassies** (public domain, powered by Wikidata).
  - Repository: https://github.com/database-of-embassies/database-of-embassies
  - Site: https://database-of-embassies.github.io/
- Rows: 1 mission(s); 0 with a published email.
- Each row carries city/address/phone/website and the Wikidata QID as a
  reference. Verify current details against the mission's own website.

## Head of State / Head of Government  (`head.of.state.txt`)
- Names (public, no personal emails published):
  - CIA World Leaders directory (public domain): https://www.cia.gov/resources/world-leaders/
  - Wikipedia — current heads of state and government:
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_current_heads_of_state_and_government
- Official contact is normally via the office's website/contact form.

## Government Ministers  (`minister.txt`)
- Names/cabinet: CIA World Leaders directory (above); the relevant ministry's
  official website. Foreign-ministry contacts can be cross-checked via the
  Lowy Global Diplomacy Index: https://globaldiplomacyindex.lowyinstitute.org/

## Governor (subnational)  (`governor.txt`)
- Subnational; source from the country's official state/province/region
  government websites. No global open email dataset exists.

## Mayoral (municipal)  (`mayoral.txt`)
- Municipal; source from individual city websites. No global open dataset of
  mayor emails is openly licensed (the US Conference of Mayors set is paid).

---
No email addresses were invented for any placeholder role. Only `ambassador.csv`
contains sourced addresses. See the top-level `contacts/national/README.md`.
