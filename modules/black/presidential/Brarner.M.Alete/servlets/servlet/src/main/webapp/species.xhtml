Animalia Classes

Warm-blooded and cold-blooded vertebrates, arthropods, and invertebrates across 5 major classes.

▶ Mammalia 153 families · 6,400 species · Jun 2026

Warm-blooded vertebrates characterized by mammary glands, hair/fur, and three middle ear bones.

▶Primates16 families · 500+ species

Includes apes, monkeys, lemurs, tarsiers. Highly developed brains and opposable thumbs.

▶Carnivora16 families · 286 species

Cats, dogs, bears, seals, weasels. Specialized carnassial teeth for shearing flesh.

▶Rodentia33 families · 2,277 species

Mice, rats, squirrels, beavers, porcupines. Continuously growing incisors.

▶Chiroptera21 families · 1,400+ species

Bats. Only mammals capable of sustained flight. Echolocation in most species.

▶Artiodactyla10 families · 550 species

Even-toed ungulates: deer, cattle, pigs, camels, hippos, whales.

▶ Aves 248 families · 10,800 species · Jun 2026

Feathered, winged, bipedal endothermic vertebrates.

▶Passeriformes140 families · 6,500+ species

Perching birds — songbirds, corvids, finches. Largest avian order.

▶Accipitriformes4 families · 266 species

Diurnal raptors — eagles, hawks, kites, vultures.

▶Psittaciformes4 families · 398 species

Parrots, macaws, cockatoos. Zygodactyl feet and curved bills.

▶Strigiformes2 families · 254 species

Owls. Nocturnal raptors with binocular vision and silent flight.

▶ Reptilia 92 families · 11,700 species · May 2026

Cold-blooded, air-breathing vertebrates with scales or scutes.

▶Squamata61 families · 10,900+ species

Lizards, snakes, amphisbaenians. Scaled reptiles with moveable quadrate bones.

▶Testudines14 families · 360 species

Turtles, tortoises, terrapins. Bony or cartilaginous shell.

▶Crocodylia3 families · 27 species

Crocodiles, alligators, gharials. Semi-aquatic apex predators.

▶ Amphibia 74 families · 8,400 species · May 2026

Ectothermic tetrapods with aquatic larval stage.

▶Anura (Frogs)55 families · 7,300+ species

Tailless amphibians with long hind limbs adapted for jumping.

▶Urodela (Salamanders)10 families · 760 species

Tailed amphibians with elongated bodies. Many capable of limb regeneration.

▶Gymnophiona (Caecilians)10 families · 214 species

Limbless, serpentine amphibians. Mostly fossorial (burrowing).

▶ Insecta 660 families · 1,000,000+ species · Apr 2026

Six-legged arthropods with compound eyes and exoskeleton.

▶Coleoptera (Beetles)166 families · 400,000+ species

Hardened forewings (elytra). Most species-rich order on Earth.

▶Lepidoptera (Moths/Butterflies)126 families · 180,000+ species

Scale-winged insects. Complete metamorphosis with caterpillar larval stage.

▶Hymenoptera (Ants/Bees/Wasps)132 families · 153,000+ species

Membrane-winged insects. Many eusocial with complex colony structures.

▶Diptera (Flies)188 families · 152,000+ species

True flies with single pair of wings. Halteres for balance in flight.

Plantae Classes

Land plants across 3 major classes — flowering, monocots, and conifers.

▶ Magnoliopsida 416 families · 300,000+ species

Flowering plants (dicots). Largest class of land plants covering trees, shrubs, herbs.

▶ Liliopsida 90 families · 70,000+ species

Monocotyledons including grasses, orchids, palms, and lilies.

▶ Pinopsida 7 families · 630 species

Conifers: pines, spruces, firs, cedars, cypresses.

Fungi Classes

Decomposers and symbionts across 2 primary classes.

▶ Agaricomycetes 120 families · 21,000+ species

Mushrooms, bracket fungi, puffballs. Primary decomposers in forest ecosystems.

▶ Sordariomycetes 90 families · 10,000+ species

Flask fungi including plant pathogens and endophytes.

Protista Classes

Unicellular eukaryotes across 2 phyla.

▶ Ciliophora 50 families · 4,500 species

Ciliated protozoans including Paramecium, Stentor, Vorticella.

▶ Apicomplexa 30 families · 6,000 species

Parasitic protists including Plasmodium (malaria) and Toxoplasma.
