Court Records, Cases & Laws (.txt & .pdf)When it comes to specific legal texts, public laws, and legal opinions, the best sources depend on the scope of your search:General U.S. Federal Laws: You can access official text and PDF publications of all three branches of the U.S. Federal Government via the Government Publishing Office GovInfo.Supreme Court & Circuit Courts: For historical US law and landmark cases digitized into accessible formats, the Harvard Law Library collection operates the Caselaw Access Project.Court Filings: Federal district and bankruptcy dockets can be searched and downloaded from the Public Access to Court Electronic Records (PACER) service. For a free, open-source alternative for finding federal and appellate case documents in text or PDF, use CourtListener.
